Sun Lab

Move the Sun and watch one lunar scene change. On the right is the “map” image of the same place, made with the Sun at azimuth 135°, elevation 30°. For every Sun position, the lab shows how the Galatea model ranks the true place among all 150 map images of this terrain. Rank 1 means it recognises the place.

Yellow: your Sun. Dashed: the map’s Sun. Further from the centre means lower in the sky.
